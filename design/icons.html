Why it works. “There is more behind this” without explaining the gesture. The chevron says “up”, which is exactly the interaction. Least distinctive of the three — it could belong to any app.

Recommendation
A, the arch. It owns the “inside” idea, keeps the mark to two shapes, and gives the product a motif that can carry into the folder card, the empty states and the onboarding.
